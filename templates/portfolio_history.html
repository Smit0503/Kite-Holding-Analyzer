Data notes

    Return % is P&L divided by the cost basis of the currently held units that had been purchased by that date. The latest chart point uses current holding prices and average cost, so it matches Holdings P&L. Equity, debt, and metal use IndianAPI historical prices matched by trading symbol or ISIN; mutual funds use historical NAVs matched to the holding ISIN. Prices are cached locally. Same-day trades follow the Holdings breakdown FIFO rule: buys are processed before sells.
